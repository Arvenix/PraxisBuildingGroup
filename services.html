Also within scope

Capabilities That Cross Service Lines

Some work does not sit in a single category. These capabilities are part of how Praxis runs projects rather than standalone products, and they show up inside the services above.

Carpentry

Rough and finish carpentry, from structural framing and stair work to trim, built ins and door and window installation.

Interior Renovations

Interior work that crosses rooms: layout changes, wall removal, ceiling work, doors, trim and paint.

Exterior Renovations

Roofing, siding, soffit and fascia, gutters, decks, porches and the water management details behind them.

Construction Management

Running a project on behalf of an owner, including scheduling, trade coordination, inspections and closeout.

Production Style Renovations

Repeatable renovation programs for owners turning over multiple units or properties on a schedule.

Custom Remodeling

One off work on houses with unusual construction, older framing or architectural details worth keeping.

Investment Property Renovation

Scope built around return: what has to be fixed, what improves value, and what can wait.

Major Property Rehabilitation

Houses that need structural, mechanical and cosmetic work before they are livable or rentable again.

Garages and Outbuildings

Detached garages, pole style buildings, workshops and storage structures.

Most Projects Are More Than One Service

A kitchen is cabinetry, but it is also electrical, plumbing, flooring, drywall and finish carpentry. A basement finish is framing, insulation, electrical, mechanical coordination and egress. Praxis contracts for the whole thing rather than the visible part.
