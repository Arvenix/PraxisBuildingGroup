Behind the photos

What a Finished Project Actually Involves

Gallery photos show the last five percent. The work that determines whether a project holds up is underneath it.

01

What is behind the wall

Framing corrections, blocking, insulation and the electrical and plumbing rough in that has to pass inspection before anything is closed up.

02

Water and air

Flashing, drainage planes, vapor control and ventilation. On exteriors and bathrooms especially, this is what separates a five year finish from a twenty year one.

03

Order of operations

Trades scheduled so nobody works over finished surfaces and nothing has to be opened back up after it has been closed.
