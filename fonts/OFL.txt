  https://openfontlicense.org

Under the OFL these fonts may be used, studied, modified and redistributed
freely, including bundled with this website, provided they are not sold by
themselves and the reserved font names are respected.

The files here were produced by the Google Fonts service and contain only the
latin and latin-ext character subsets in order to keep transfer size small.
To regenerate them, request the families from fonts.googleapis.com with a
modern browser user agent and save the woff2 files the returned stylesheet
references.
