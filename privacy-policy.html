1. Who This Policy Covers

This policy applies to praxisbg.com, operated by Praxis Building Group, LLC ("Praxis", "we", "us"). It describes what information the website collects, why, and what is done with it.

2. Information You Provide

The website collects information only when you choose to send it, primarily through the consultation form. That may include your name, email address, phone number, project address, city, project type, budget range, timeline, preferred contact method and whatever you write in the project description.

Do not send sensitive personal information through the website form. It is not needed to discuss a construction project.

3. Why We Collect It

Information submitted through the site is used to respond to your inquiry, schedule a consultation, prepare scope and pricing, and communicate about a project. It is not sold.

5. Cookies

The Praxis website does not set cookies of its own for advertising or profiling. Third party analytics or advertising tags, if installed, may set cookies. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies in their settings.

6. How Information Is Shared

Praxis shares inquiry information only as needed to respond to it. That may include service providers such as email hosting, form processing or a customer relationship system, and trade partners where necessary to price or perform work you have requested. Praxis may also disclose information where required by law.

[SPECIFY RETENTION PERIOD.] Inquiry and project records are retained for as long as needed to serve the project and to satisfy business, tax, warranty and legal record keeping requirements.
